### 5. Administration
The activation of a mailbox or forwarding, the switch from mailbox to forwarding or vice versa, as well as the deactivation is done by yourself under _**MyVDT > Email > "tonmeisterei" Email administration**_ tab **Usage**.

**for users of the option mailbox**
You manage your mailbox yourself. To do this, log in to the [webmailer](https://webmail.all-inkl.com/) with your access data. Here you have access to all settings and your emails. Alternatively, you can manage your emails using an email program. You will find a link to the webmailer, your access data and other setting parameters after activating the mailbox under _**MyVDT > Email > "tonmeisterei" Email administration**_ tab **E-Mail Application Setup**.

Further information, e.g. Set-up aids for your e-mail program can be found in the FAQs.
